## Technical Overview & Enterprise Diagnostic Walkthrough

🟪 Windows Tips & Tricks – Part 4

🌐 Find Your IP Address in Seconds

Need your IP for troubleshooting?

🛠️ Method:
⌨️ Press Windows + R
Type: cmd
Then: ipconfig
Look for: IPv4 Address

🎯 Why this matters:
✅ Network troubleshooting
✅ Printer setup
✅ Remote access configuration
✅ DNS diagnostics

💡 Pro Tip:
Use: ipconfig /all for detailed network information.
