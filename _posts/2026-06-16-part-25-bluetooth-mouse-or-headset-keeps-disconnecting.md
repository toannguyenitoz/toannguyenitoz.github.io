## Technical Overview & Enterprise Diagnostic Walkthrough

🟥 Windows Tips & Tricks – Part 25

🖱️ Bluetooth Mouse or Headset Keeps Disconnecting?

Before replacing the device, check Windows Power Management settings.

🔧 Quick Fix

Windows + X → Device Manager

➡️ Bluetooth or USB Controllers

➡️ Properties → Power Management

➡️ Uncheck:

☑️ Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power

➡️ Click OK

✅ Fix random Bluetooth dropouts

✅ Improve mouse and headset responsiveness

✅ Prevent USB devices from sleeping

💡 Sometimes the problem isn't the device—it's Windows trying too hard to save power.
