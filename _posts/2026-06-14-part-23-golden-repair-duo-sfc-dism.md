## Technical Overview & Practical Protocol

⚡ [WINDOWS TIPS & TRICKS - PART 23] GOLDEN REPAIR DUO: SFC & DISM 🚀

"How do I resolve and optimize golden repair duo: sfc & dism?"

Run DISM followed by SFC to download clean replacement Windows binaries and repair corrupted system files.

📌 KEY TAKEAWAYS & PROTOCOL:
• Category: System Performance
• Approach: Root-cause diagnostic & enterprise-ready efficiency.
• Execution: Follow the exact step-by-step layout shown in the infographic.

💡 PRO SYSADMIN TIP:
Always run DISM first so SFC can pull fresh healthy files from component store.
