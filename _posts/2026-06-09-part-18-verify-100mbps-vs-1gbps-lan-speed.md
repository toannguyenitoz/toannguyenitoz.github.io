## Technical Overview & Practical Protocol

⚡ [WINDOWS TIPS & TRICKS - PART 18] VERIFY 100MBPS VS 1GBPS LAN SPEED 🚀

"Gigabit fiber internet only downloading at 95Mbps on your desktop PC?"

Check physical Ethernet adapter link negotiation speed — a damaged 8-pin wire drops link speed from 1000Mbps to 100Mbps.

📌 KEY TAKEAWAYS & PROTOCOL:
• Category: Network Troubleshooting
• Approach: Root-cause diagnostic & enterprise-ready efficiency.
• Execution: Follow the exact step-by-step layout shown in the infographic.

💡 PRO SYSADMIN TIP:
Cat5e/Cat6 cables need all 8 pins working for Gigabit. If even 1 pin is crimped poorly, NIC drops to 100Mbps.
