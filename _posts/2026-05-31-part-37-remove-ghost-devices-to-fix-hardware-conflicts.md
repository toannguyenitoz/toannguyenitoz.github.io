A USB dock, monitor, printer, or Bluetooth headset suddenly stops working—even though it works perfectly on another PC?
Windows may be holding onto old hidden device profiles.
- How to Reveal Hidden Devices
⌨️ Press Windows + X
➡️ Open Device Manager
➡️ Click View
➡️ Select Show hidden devices
🎯 Why IT Pros Use It
- Fix USB and docking station conflicts
- Resolve Bluetooth pairing issues
- Remove old printer profiles
- Force Windows to create a fresh hardware connection
🚀 Pro Tip
After enabling Show hidden devices, look for faded or translucent device icons.
Right-click the old device ➜ Uninstall device
🔄 Reboot the PC and reconnect the hardware.
- Sometimes the device causing the problem is the one you can't see.
