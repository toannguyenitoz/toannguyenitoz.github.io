## The Inefficient vs. The Enterprise Method

### ❌ The Common Inefficient Approach (Avoid)
- **One Cluttered Desktop**: All 20 apps on one screen, constantly Alt-Tabbing and losing focus.
- **Manual Window Resizing**: Wasting time dragging and resizing windows to try to separate tasks.
- **Closing Apps to Free Space**: Closing important apps just to see another task, then reopening them.

### ✅ The Best-Practice SysAdmin Method (Adopt)
- **Win + Ctrl + D = New Desktop**: Create a separate virtual desktop for Work, Personal, and Projects.
- **Win + Ctrl + Arrows = Switch**: Slide between desktops instantly without losing any open windows.
- **Task View to Manage All**: Press Win + Tab to see all virtual desktops and drag apps between them.

## 💡 20+ Years Enterprise IT Pro-Tip

> **Toan's Pro-Tip:** Right-click a virtual desktop thumbnail in Task View (Win+Tab) and rename it (e.g. 'PROD SERVER', 'TICKETS', 'RESEARCH').
