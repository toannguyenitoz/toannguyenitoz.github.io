💬 “My laptop is running, but both external monitors are completely black!”

Before holding down the power button, try this simple Windows shortcut:

⌨️ Ctrl + Shift + Windows + B

🔄 Windows will briefly reset the graphics driver.

You may hear a short beep and see the screen flash. Your applications and files should remain open.

🎯 Why it's useful

- Black screen after docking
- External monitor not responding
- Display flickering or freezing
- No need to reboot the PC
- No need to close your applications

- Pro Tip

Try this shortcut before reaching for the power button.

Reset the display driver — not the entire PC. 🔥
