## Technical Overview & Practical Protocol

⚡ [WINDOWS TIPS & TRICKS - PART 12] REVEAL SAVED WI-FI PASSWORDS 🚀

"Need to connect another device to Wi-Fi but don't know the password saved on your PC?"

Use netsh wlan to extract the plaintext password of any saved Wi-Fi network on your Windows PC in seconds.

📌 KEY TAKEAWAYS & PROTOCOL:
• Category: Network Troubleshooting
• Approach: Root-cause diagnostic & enterprise-ready efficiency.
• Execution: Follow the exact step-by-step layout shown in the infographic.

💡 PRO SYSADMIN TIP:
Run 'netsh wlan show profiles' first to see all saved network SSIDs stored on the workstation.
