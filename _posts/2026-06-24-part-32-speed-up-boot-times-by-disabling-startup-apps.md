## Technical Overview & Enterprise Diagnostic Walkthrough

🟥 Windows Tips & Tricks – Part 32

🚀 Speed Up Boot Times by Disabling Startup Apps

PC takes forever to become usable after login?

Check which apps are launching automatically with Windows.

🔧 Quick Fix

⌨️ Ctrl + Shift + Esc

➡️ Open Task Manager

➡️ Select Startup apps

➡️ Sort by Startup impact

➡️ Disable non-essential apps

🎯 Why IT Pros Use It

✅ Faster boot times

✅ More free RAM and CPU

✅ Less background clutter

✅ Instant performance boost

🚀 Pro Tip

Focus on apps marked High under Startup Impact.

⚠️ Avoid disabling antivirus, drivers, or business-critical software.

💡 A clean startup list is one of the easiest ways to speed up Windows.
