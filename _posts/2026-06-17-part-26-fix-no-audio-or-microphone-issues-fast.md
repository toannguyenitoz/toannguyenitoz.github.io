## Technical Overview & Enterprise Diagnostic Walkthrough

🟥 Windows Tips & Tricks – Part 26

🎧 Fix “No Audio” or Microphone Issues Fast

Headset not working in Teams or Zoom?

Open the classic Sound Control Panel and fix audio routing quickly.

🔧 How to Open

⌨️ Windows + R

➡️ Type:

mmsys.cpl

➡️ Press Enter

🎯 Why IT Pros Use It

✅ Set the correct default speaker or headset

✅ Set the correct default microphone

✅ Test mic levels and boost

✅ Disable ghost audio devices

✅ Turn off Audio Enhancements if voice sounds robotic

🚀 Pro Tip

Using HDMI or DisplayPort monitors?

Windows may route sound to the monitor instead of the headset.

Open mmsys.cpl, right-click the monitor audio device, and select Disable.

💡 Classic Control Panel tools are still the fastest way to troubleshoot Windows audio.
