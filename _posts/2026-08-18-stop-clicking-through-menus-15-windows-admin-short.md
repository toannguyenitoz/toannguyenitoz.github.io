- Stop Clicking Through Menus: 15 Windows Admin Shortcuts Every IT Pro Needs

In IT Support and System Administration, efficiency is everything. When an urgent ticket comes in, navigating 4 or 5 levels deep into Windows Settings wastes precious minutes.

Pressing [ Windows Key + R ] and typing direct MMC and CPL shortcuts gets you straight to the root of the problem:

- Investigating a blue screen? ➔ eventvwr.msc
- Configuring network adapters or DNS? ➔ ncpa.cpl
- Checking stuck print spoolers or SQL services? ➔ services.msc
- Managing disk partitions & volumes? ➔ diskmgmt.msc
- Cleanly removing stubborn software? ➔ appwiz.cpl
