## Technical Overview & Practical Protocol

⚡ [WINDOWS TIPS & TRICKS - PART 63] WIN + P: DUAL DISPLAY PROJECTION MODES 🚀

"How do I resolve and optimize win + p: dual display projection modes?"

Press Win + P to cycle between Duplicate, Extend, and Second Screen Only.
