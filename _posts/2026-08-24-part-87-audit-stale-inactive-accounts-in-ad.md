## Technical Overview & Practical Protocol

⚡ [WINDOWS TIPS & TRICKS - PART 87] AUDIT STALE INACTIVE ACCOUNTS IN AD 🚀

"How do I resolve and optimize audit stale inactive accounts in ad?"

Search-ADAccount -AccountInactive -TimeSpan 90.00:00:00 -UsersOnly.

📌 KEY TAKEAWAYS & PROTOCOL:
• Category: Active Directory & Domain
• Approach: Root-cause diagnostic & enterprise-ready efficiency.
• Execution: Follow the exact step-by-step layout shown in the infographic.

💡 PRO SYSADMIN TIP:
Disable accounts inactive for over 90 days to prevent dormant exploits.
