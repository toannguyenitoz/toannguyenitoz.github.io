📧 Outlook Troubleshooting – Quick Reference Guide

If you work in IT Support, you've probably answered the same Outlook questions hundreds of times:

❓ "Why won't my email send?" 
❓ "Outlook keeps crashing." 
❓ "My mailbox is full." 
❓ "Why is Outlook not responding?" 
❓ "How do I add a Shared Mailbox?"

To save time for both IT teams and end users, I created this Outlook Troubleshooting Quick Guide—a one-page reference covering some of the most common Outlook issues and their first-line solutions.

It includes quick steps for:

- Adding a Shared Mailbox (Classic Outlook)

- Archiving emails to a PST file

- Troubleshooting emails that won't send or receive

- Recovering from Outlook crashes

- Fixing "Not Responding" issues

- Managing oversized OST files

- Compacting OST files to improve performance

Whether you're an IT Support Engineer, Service Desk Analyst, Desktop Support Technician, or System Administrator, having a simple troubleshooting guide like this can significantly reduce repetitive tickets and improve first-call resolution.

Sometimes, the best documentation is the one users can understand in under two minutes.
