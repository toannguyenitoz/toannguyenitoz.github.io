## The Inefficient vs. The Enterprise Method

### ❌ The Common Inefficient Approach (Avoid)
- **Manual Minimize Loop**: Clicking the minimize button 20 times to clear background windows.
- **Win + D Reset**: Minimizing everything then searching for the one document you were editing.
- **Distraction Overload**: Leaving cluttered windows open and losing train of thought.

### ✅ The Best-Practice SysAdmin Method (Adopt)
- **Grab Active Title Bar**: Left-click and hold the title bar of the window you want to keep.
- **Quick Shake Left & Right**: Shake mouse back and forth — all other windows instantly minimize!
- **Shake Again to Restore**: Shake title bar again to bring all background windows back instantly.

---

## 💡 20+ Years Enterprise IT Pro-Tip

> **Toan's Pro-Tip:** Enable this under Settings > System > Multitasking > Title bar window shake if disabled on your Windows 11 build.
