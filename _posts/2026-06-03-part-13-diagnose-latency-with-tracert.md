## Technical Overview & Practical Protocol

⚡ [WINDOWS TIPS & TRICKS - PART 13] DIAGNOSE LATENCY WITH TRACERT 🚀

"Internet feels slow or dropping packets — is the issue your PC, router, or ISP?"

Use tracert and ping to identify exactly which routing hop is causing latency spikes or packet drops.

• Category: Network Troubleshooting
• Approach: Root-cause diagnostic & enterprise-ready efficiency.
