## Technical Overview & Practical Protocol

⚡ [WINDOWS TIPS & TRICKS - PART 83] QUERY AD USER GROUP MEMBERSHIP WITH POWERSHELL 🚀

"How do I resolve and optimize query ad user group membership with powershell?"

Run Get-ADPrincipalGroupMembership username | Select-Object Name.
