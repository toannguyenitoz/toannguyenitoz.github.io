## Step-by-Step Implementation Procedure

### Step 1: Hold SHIFT key while clicking Start > Power > Restart
Press: <kbd>Shift</kbd>

### Step 2: On blue screen, select 'Troubleshoot' > 'Advanced options'
Execute this configuration carefully in accordance with your organization's endpoint policy.

### Step 3: Click 'Startup Settings' and hit 'Restart' button
Execute this configuration carefully in accordance with your organization's endpoint policy.

### Step 4: Press '4' on keyboard for Safe Mode or '5' for Safe Mode with Networking
Press: <kbd>4</kbd>
