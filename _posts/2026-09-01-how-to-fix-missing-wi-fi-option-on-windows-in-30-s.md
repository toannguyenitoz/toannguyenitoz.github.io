![How to Fix Missing Wi-Fi Option on Windows in 30 Seconds](/assets/images/posts/how-to-fix-missing-wi-fi-option-on-windows-in-30-s.jpg)

If your Wi-Fi button suddenly disappears from your Windows PC, try this quick fix:
- Press Windows + R
- Type: services.msc and press Enter
- Find: WLAN AutoConfig
- Right-click → Properties
- Change Startup type to Automatic
- Click Start
- Select Apply → OK
🎉 Done! Your Wi-Fi option may reappear immediately.
- ️ Still not working? Check Device Manager and make sure your wireless adapter is enabled and working properly.
- This fix works when the WLAN AutoConfig service is disabled or stopped.
