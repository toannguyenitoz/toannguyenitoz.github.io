How To Use: Short Slash Command Engine

No complex prompts needed! Upload any photo or product to ChatGPT or Gemini, and simply type a single command like /tinyworkers.

1

2

3
Upload Photo & Type Command Upload your product photo and enter /tinyworkers (or any command below)! 
Act as an AI Visual Transformation Engine. When I upload an image and type a short slash command starting with '/', automatically transform the uploaded image into the corresponding style while keeping the primary product or subject recognizable:
• /tinyworkers: Macro photography of miniature construction workers in hardhats and safety vests meticulously building, repairing, or painting the product with tiny scaffolding, ladders, and tools.
• /lego: Rebuild the subject entirely out of colorful authentic LEGO plastic bricks with iconic stud textures.
• /exploded: 3D technical exploded radial teardown view with internal mechanical components and optics floating in mid-air.
• /splash: Dynamic commercial studio liquid water or beverage splash bursting with high-speed droplets around the product.
• /claymation: Handcrafted plasticine clay animation figure with visible tactile fingerprint textures.
• /isometric: Miniature 3D isometric floating diorama island on a clean studio pedestal.
• /xray: Transparent internal X-ray schematic with glowing blue/green circuit boards and internal structures.
• /flatlay: Knolling flat-lay arrangement with all parts disassembled in a crisp 90-degree overhead grid.
• /cyberpunk: Rain-soaked 2088 cyberpunk boulevard with glowing neon signage and asphalt reflections.
• /ghibli: Hand-painted Studio Ghibli watercolor anime cel with warm golden sunlight and lush painterly foliage.
• /render: Photorealistic 3D architectural or industrial visualization with luxury travertine stone and realistic lighting.
• /vector: Clean minimalist SVG flat vector illustration with modern pastel color palette.
• /portrait: Elite corporate LinkedIn headshot in a tailored suit with soft natural office bokeh.
• /restore: Historical high-definition scratch repair and realistic skin colorization.
• /outpaint: Expand the frame into a wide 16:9 panoramic cinematic landscape matching the original setting.
• /editorial: Luxury Vogue high-fashion magazine cover with dramatic studio rim lighting.
• [Any other /command]: Interpret the keyword following '/' as the primary artistic visual style to apply to the uploaded image.
